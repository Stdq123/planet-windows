# Code of conduct

We want a welcoming and constructive community. Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and focus discussion on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer through a channel you already have; for a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled with discretion where practical.
